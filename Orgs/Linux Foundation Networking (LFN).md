
## Stages

*Retrieved from [LFN Lifecycle States & Guidelines](https://lf-networking.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LN/pages/716406785/LFN+Lifecycle+States+Guidelines)* 

1. **Candidate:** Project looking to join the LFN group. They get support from the LFN.
2. **Sandbox:** For early stage projects that are new and or emerging. They get constant engagement and hands-on support.
3. **Incubation:** Projects that follow best practices and adhere to governance principles of the LFN. They should cooperate with other LFN projects. (Similar to [[Apache Incubator]] I think?). They get moderate support from other LFN staff, but are expected to be self managed.
4. **Graduated:** Projects with established communities and constant, stable operation. They comply with open-source best practices 
5. **Maintenance or LTS:** Feature-complete projects. They receive bug fixes, security updates, and support, but slower development.
6. **Archived:** Projects that don't have an active community or have not received commits in the previous 12 months.
